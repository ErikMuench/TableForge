About Idle Farm

Idle Farm is a cozy idle farming game developed by Tableforge. Build and expand your farm, grow crops, collect resources, discover new characters and progress through the game at your own pace.

Idle Farm includes optional Google Play Games features such as Google Play Games sign-in, achievements and leaderboards.
