Privacy Policy

Idle Farm

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tableforge ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Idle Farm.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide and improve the game and its optional features.

2. Google Play Games Services

Idle Farm may use Google Play Games Services to provide optional features such as Google Play Games sign-in, achievements and leaderboards.

When you use Google Play Games Services, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games profile, including your gamer identity, game scores, achievements, leaderboard information and certain device or account identifiers.

Depending on your Google account and Play Games settings, Google may also process information such as your account name or other account information.

This information is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Games' applicable policies.

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Advertising

Idle Farm uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Advertisements may include optional rewarded advertisements that users can choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes.

The information processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's applicable policies and privacy practices.

More information about Google's use of data in advertising services is available at:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, Idle Farm may request your consent before personalized advertising or certain other forms of data processing are used.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be presented with privacy and advertising consent options where required by applicable law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed in connection with Idle Farm.

Data transmitted by third-party services used by the game, including Google services, is handled according to the security practices of those services.

6. Children's Privacy

Idle Farm is not specifically directed at children under the applicable minimum age for online services in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for our own purposes.
