About Idle Farm

Idle Farm is a cozy idle farming game developed by Tableforge.

In Idle Farm, players build and expand their own farm, grow crops, raise animals, collect resources, earn money and progress through the game over time.

The game is designed as a relaxed idle experience. Players can develop their farm and continue making progress even when they are not actively playing.

Game Features

  • Build and expand your own farm
  • Grow and harvest different crops
  • Raise and manage farm animals
  • Collect and produce various resources
  • Earn money and improve your farm
  • Progress through the game's advancement system
  • Unlock achievements and track your progress
